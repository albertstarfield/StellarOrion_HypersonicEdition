Protocol to Utilize the Thesis 

Unpublished Master’s Thesis is registered and provided in the Library of Institut 

Teknologi Bandung, can be accessed by any person with respect that the ownership 

of the Thesis is belong to the Author by complying the Intellectual Property Rghts 

(HaKI) applied in Institut Teknologi Bandung. For literature reference, the Thesis 

can  be  recorded,  but  to  cite  the  Thesis,  it  is  only  allowed  if  the  Author  gives 

permission and it should follow the scientific code. 

Citation  of  research  result  of  this  Thesis  may  be  written  in  Bahasa  Indonesia  as 

follows, 

Tantono,  A.D.  (2021):  Studi  Parametrik  Performa  Aerodinamis  Sayap  Kepak 

Fleksibel  Dan  Akustik  Modal  Dengan  Variasi  Sifat  Material  Dan 

Morfologi, Tesis Program Magister, Institut Teknologi Bandung. 

In English, it may be written as follows, 

Tantono, A.D. (2021): Parametric Study of Flexible Flapping Wing Aerodynamic 

Performance and Modal Acoustic with Material Properties and Morphology 

Variation, Master’s Thesis, Institut Teknologi Bandung. 

Copying or publishing a part of or complete Thesis should be under the permission 

of The Dean of Graduate School of Institut Teknologi Bandung.

Acknowledgement 

This thesis is all done thanks to the Lord who has lent me the strength to do it. So 

let  me  introduce  myself.  I  have  finished  my  undergraduate  study  at  Institut 

Teknologi  Bandung  in  2019  with  similar  bachelor  thesis  topic,  flapping  wing. 

Through the joy and sorrow memories is passed by until I finally come to the final 

year here in Aerospace engineering postgraduate school at FTMD ITB. 

I would like to express my sincere gratitude to Mr Mochammad Agoes Moelyadi 

as my supervisor who has also inspire me about aerodynamic field. He gave me 

supports to study in the flying bird that actually an interesting yet complex problem. 

Step by step simulation, in all of success and vain, he has teach me the basic about 

how to simulate transient flow in ANSYS. Some of ANSYS help itself also comes 

in handy. I also send my gratitude to Mr Javensius Sembiring as lecturer who has 

already be patient with my weakness in writing this thesis to become unblemished 

according to FTMD ITB template.    

Thanks  to  my  family  who  never  cease  to  support  me,  morally  and  substantially. 

Then  my  Sion  fellow  friends  and  HALE  UAV  ITB  teams,  especially  Yeremia 

Kristianto that accompanying me during my last month on this thesis work. Also, 

my junior mates in Flapping Wing UAV ITB teams who collaborate with me to 

make feasible flapping wing UAV that all we hope is flying soon.      

Well, I personally hope this thesis would help people to understand a little bit about 

dynamic simulation and especially the flexible flapping case. And I hope this thesis 

may help to create a flapping wing MAV in future. Thank you.
