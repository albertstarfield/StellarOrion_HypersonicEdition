[BODY] OBJECTIVE AND RESEARCH SCOPE
[BODY] Recall: the Objective and the Scope
[BODY] RECALL
[BODY] The HIAD as an overview: the inflatable aeroshell against the rigid body it replaces, as the thesis draws it.
[BODY] Source: Hiad_DesignSpec.png from CurrentThesisFinalReport/figures, the vehicle specification drawing of Chapter III.
[BODY] • THE OBJECTIVE: build and validate one framework that predicts a HIAD aerothermal load and optimises its geometry for survivability, using SPARTA DSMC and a genetic algorithm.
[BODY] • THE SCOPE: rigid, fully inflated 6-toroid HIAD; 5-species air with VSS collisions; 0.01 < Kn < 1 at 50 to 60 km; 2D axisymmetric or 3D; GA on an analytic cost.
[BODY] • WHAT RIGID MEANS: the fully inflated shape is frozen for the run: no pressure-load deflection, no fabric wrinkling, no ablation or recession, and no fluid-structure coupling.
[BODY] • HOW IT IS CHECKED: Sutton-Graves and SPARTA surface quantities against IRVE-3 flight data, a four-level grid independence study, and a fixed seed 42 for re-runs.
[BODY] Out of scope: packing and deployment mechanics, toroid structural sizing and the PINN path; the FTPS itself is flexible, only its shape is frozen.

[BODY] Where the fourth power comes from:
The surface sheds heat by re-radiating, q_rad = eps*sigma*T^4. Conduction carries the flux through the stack; radiation fixes the temperature at which that flux leaves, so T^4 is the boundary condition of the conduction problem, not a conduction law.

[BODY] WHY T^4 SITS OUTSIDE FOURIER
[BODY] Issues: Issue 3, where the fourth power comes from
[BODY] RECALL
[BODY] Where the fourth power is born:
Planck's spectral exitance integrated over every frequency gives q = sigma*T^4; the fourth power is the moment of x^3/(e^x - 1), pi^4/15, with zeta(4) = pi^4/90. It is an integral, not a fitted exponent.
[BODY] Why it is not a second conduction term:
Fourier carries heat into the stack; Stefan-Boltzmann carries it out to space. At one wall node these are two parallel sinks sharing one T, each counted once: (T - T_back)/R + eps*sigma*(T^4 - T_inf^4) = q_w. Folding sigma*T^4 into k would spend the same photons twice.
[BODY] When radiation joins the conduction term:
the flux diffuses as q_rad = -k_R grad T, with k_R = 16*sigma*T^3/(3*kappa_R), a volumetric conductivity rather than a wall flux. It holds only in an optically thick medium; the shock layer here is not, so the term is dropped.
[BODY] What the solve then faces:
f(T) = (T - T_back)/R + eps*sigma*(T^4 - T_inf^4) - q_w + G*T - S. The slope 1/R + 4*eps*sigma*T^3 + G is positive, so the root is unique; that 4*eps*sigma*T^3 is the tangent of the same quartic, 417 W/m2 per kelvin at 1,348 K, not a new term.
[BODY] One radiative term at the wall, one conductive term through the stack, two parallel paths at one node.
