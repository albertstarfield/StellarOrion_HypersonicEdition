\chapter{Conclusion and Recommendations}

\section{Summary of Findings}
This research developed and validated a computational framework for aerothermal analysis and survivability optimization of Hypersonic Inflatable Aerodynamic Decelerators (HIADs) using the SPARTA Direct Simulation Monte Carlo (DSMC) solver.

\section{Key Conclusions}

\subsection{DSMC Validation Against IRVE-3 Flight Data}
The SPARTA DSMC simulation framework was validated against the IRVE-3 post-flight aerothermal reconstruction data~\cite{old2013irve3postflight}. The Fay-Riddell stagnation-point correlation predicts a peak heat flux of 13.83\,W/cm$^2$, which is within 3.7\% of the 14.36\,W/cm$^2$ flight measurement~\cite{rapisarda2023mdao}. The Sutton-Graves correlation~\cite{sutton1971gravesh}, evaluated at the DSMC run condition with the corrected coefficient $C_{SG} = 1.83 \times 10^{-4}$ and the IRVE-3 nose radius $R_N = 1.5$\,m, gives a stagnation-point heat flux of 16.14\,W/cm$^2$. That value is $+12.4\%$ relative to the flight measurement and $+5.8\%$ relative to Rapisarda's trajectory-integrated Sutton-Graves peak of 15.26\,W/cm$^2$, itself $+6.26\%$ above flight~\cite{rapisarda2023mdao}. The quoted value conditions the correlation on the DSMC run rather than a raw sample, and it agrees closely with Rapisarda's trajectory-integrated value.

\subsection{Scalloping-Induced Heating Augmentation}
The toroidal surface topology creates a periodic heating profile with enhanced peaks at the toroid crests. Against the smooth-body equivalent at the same trajectory condition, the scalloped configuration raises peak heat flux by 15.2\% and the per-element average by 4.0\%, consistent with the inverse-square-root radius relationship ($\dot{q} \propto 1/\sqrt{R}$) of the Sutton-Graves correlation. The recirculation zones in the valleys between toroidal rings provide partial thermal shielding, reducing the time-averaged heat load on the FTPS~\cite{hollis2018surface}.

\subsection{Flow Regime Characterization}
The Knudsen number field mapping demonstrates a systematic \emph{local} regime gradient around the HIAD at 51.8\,km altitude. The freestream and stagnation region remain in the continuum band ($Kn \lesssim 0.01$, with $Kn_{\text{stag}} \sim 0.005$), while the shock layer and wake rise into the slip-flow band ($0.01 \lesssim Kn \lesssim 0.1$). Neither the transitional band ($0.1 \lesssim Kn \lesssim 10$) nor the free-molecular band ($Kn > 10$) is attained here; both need substantially higher altitudes. This gradient justifies the solver choice: the region dominating convective heating lies where continuum Navier-Stokes requires slip corrections~\cite{bird1994molecular, plimpton2014sparta}. A single vehicle-scale $Kn$ on the 3\,m aeroshell diameter would place the whole vehicle in the continuum band ($Kn \approx 2.5\times10^{-5}$) and conceal this distinction. The local field, not the global ratio, is therefore the meaningful diagnostic, and the continuum PINN stage and the kinetic DSMC stage are complementary rather than redundant.

\subsection{Grid Independence and Computational Efficiency}
A grid factor of 0.7 gives the best balance of accuracy and cost, with the least error against validated flight data. The Cartesian grid keeps cell size below the local mean free path in the shock layer, satisfying the DSMC resolution requirement.

\subsection{Real-Gas Effects and 5-Species Air Model}
A realistic 5-species air model (N$_2$, O$_2$, NO, N, O) with Variable Soft Sphere (VSS) collision physics captures vibrational excitation and dissociation, reducing the effective stagnation temperature below the calorically perfect prediction of 5952\,K. Energy partitioning into internal modes affects the temperature gradient driving surface heat transfer~\cite{AndersonHypersonic}, which is critical for accurate heat flux prediction.

\section{Contributions}
This research contributes the following to hypersonic aerothermal analysis:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Integrated DSMC-HIAD Framework:} A complete pipeline from parametric geometry generation through DSMC simulation to survivability optimization for inflatable aerodynamic decelerators in the transitional flow regime.

    \item \textbf{Scalloping Effect Quantification:} First systematic DSMC-based characterization of the heating augmentation and recirculation patterns caused by the stacked toroid surface topology of HIADs.

    \item \textbf{PINN-DSMC Hybrid Approach:} Demonstration of Physics-Informed Neural Networks as a post-processing smoothing layer for DSMC particle data, anchored in the compressible Navier-Stokes equations.

    \item \textbf{Multi-Objective Optimization:} Automated GA-based optimization of HIAD geometry using a Gaussian Process surrogate (Mat\'ern 5/2 kernel with Expected Improvement), targeting ballistic coefficient, peak deceleration, backface temperature, and drag performance simultaneously.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Recommendations for Future Work}
The study's limitations yield these recommendations:

\subsection{Extended Trajectory Analysis}
The study covers one trajectory point at 51.8\,km altitude. Extend it to a full reentry trajectory, capturing time-evolution of aerothermal loads across atmospheric conditions and flow regimes.

\subsection{Full 3D Off-Axis Simulations}
Results come from 2D axisymmetric cases at zero angle of attack. Full 3D runs at non-zero angle of attack would capture asymmetric heating and side forces relevant to vehicle stability.

\subsection{Structural FSI Coupling}
The HIAD is treated as rigid and fully inflated. Coupling DSMC aerothermal loads to a structural finite element model would enable fluid-structure interaction (FSI) analysis of deformation and its effect on the flow field.

\subsection{Gen-2 and Gen-3 TPS Material Assessment}
The baseline is the IRVE-3 Gen-1 TPS (Nextel/Pyrogel). Evaluate thermal margins for Gen-2 (SiC/Carbon) and Gen-3 TPS materials under the predicted scalloped heating profiles, especially for Mars entry where atmospheric composition differs from Earth~\cite{guidotti2023efesto2materials}.

\subsection{Mars Entry Extension}
Extend the framework to Mars entry with a CO$_2$-dominated composition and updated SPARTA gas model. Replace the Sutton-Graves correlation with the West and Brandis (2020) Mars correlation, and the 5-species Earth air model with a CO$_2$/N$_2$/Ar mixture.

\subsection{PINN Refinement Validation}
PINN refinement gives smooth, physics-consistent interpolation of noisy DSMC data, but its accuracy across the continuum and slip-flow bands ($Kn \lesssim 0.1$) needs quantification. Compare raw DSMC and PINN-refined outputs across Knudsen numbers to establish reliability bounds.
