[card:recall-latar-belakang#1] • Inflatable aerodynamic decelerators (IADs and HIADs) pack into a small volume and inflate before entry: their large surface area gives high drag at high altitude, where the air is thin and the heating is milder.

===== slide 7 =====
[frame_title] METHODOLOGY: THE SIMULATION SETUP
[section_chip] RECALL
[figure_caption] The SPARTA computational grid of the reference run, at the grid factor 0.7 that the grid-independence study selected.
The geometry: the parametric HIAD is a body of revolution carrying a toroidal stack, and the design vector is the body radius, the torus minor radius, the half-cone angle, the toroid count and the vehicle mass.
The grid: the solver runs on a two-dimensional Cartesian grid whose spacing is finer than the local mean free path, so the shock structure and the boundary layer gradients are resolved.
The chain: the geometry and the flight point feed the SPARTA solver in Docker and the Ada/SPARK one-dimensional layered thermal solver, and the genetic algorithm writes the next geometry back.
The air: the solver carries a five-species atmosphere with variable soft sphere collisions, so the shock layer dissociates and the wall is fully catalytic.
[figure_source] Source: CurrentThesisFinalReport/figures/grid_mesh_map_smooth.png, thesis figure archive, the computational grid figure of chapter 04.
[slot_footer] How the simulation is set up. The narration stays editable in PowerPoint.
===== slide 8 =====
[frame_title] METHODOLOGY: THE GRID INDEPENDENCE
[section_chip] RECALL
[figure_caption] The grid-independence study of the SPARTA mesh: three refinement levels compared on the peak surface flux, with the mesh-quality gates of the Cartesian grid quoted as numbers.
The refinement: three levels were run at 70, 100 and 139 grid points, that is 4,900, 10,000 and 19,321 cells, carrying 0.60, 0.85 and 1.19 million particles.
The verdict: the finest pair moves the peak surface flux by 8.30 percent, inside the 10 percent tolerance the owner set on 2026-10-16.
The mesh quality: the grid is axis aligned, so the skewness is 0 and the orthogonal quality is 1 in every cell, and the aspect ratio of 3.56 is reported but not gated.
The caveat: the quantity is the peak surface kinetic-energy flux of the particle solver, a per-cell sampling maximum, and the levels carry different particle counts, so the measured change mixes discretisation with sampling.
[figure_source] Source: CurrentThesisFinalReport/figures/sop_grid_independence.png, built from the run artefacts of stellarorion_program_proc/results/99_shared/sop by the CFD standard operating procedure.

===== slide 10 =====
[frame_title] METHODOLOGY: THE CONVECTION CHANNEL
[section_chip] RECALL
[figure_caption] The Sutton-Graves correlation that carries the convective load of every candidate geometry.
The source: q = C_SG sqrt(rho/R_N) V^3, with C_SG = 1.7415e-4 for Earth air.
The magnitude: at the reference point of 3378 m/s it gives 1.536e5 W/m^2, that is 15.36 W/cm^2, for a 1.5 m nose radius.
The shape: the particle solver gives no absolute heat flux but the distribution over the surface, so its kinetic energy is kept only as a signal that ranks geometries against each other.
The nose radius: the optimiser evaluates the same correlation at the physical nose radius of the candidate, so the screening flux at the baseline is 25.36 W/cm^2.
[figure_source] Source: sutton_graves_parametric.png from CurrentThesisFinalReport/figures, the correlation figure of chapter 03.
[slot_footer] The first of the three heat-flux channels. The narration stays editable in PowerPoint.
===== slide 11 =====
[frame_title] METHODOLOGY: THE CONDUCTION CHANNEL
[section_chip] RECALL
[figure_caption] The through-thickness and per-ply thermal response of the FTPS layup at the final step: the temperature profiles of five surface elements of the reference run and the per-ply temperatures of the first element.
The path: the heat that enters the hot face is conducted inwards through the five-ply layup, one dimension along the stack, element by element.
The resistance: the stack resistance is the sum of the ply resistances, 0.3056 m^2K/W over the 3648 surface elements.
The insulation: most of the stack is the Pyrogel blanket, so the drop across the layup is large, the back face stays at 300 K and the hot face reaches 1327.2 K.
The lateral term: the in-plane coupling is closed with a lumped conductance between neighbouring elements, and it carries 0.0083 W/m^2, about six parts in a hundred million of the load.
[figure_source] Source: CurrentThesisFinalReport/figures/tps_ply_conduction.png, thesis figure archive, the ply conduction figure of chapter 04.

===== slide 12 =====
[frame_title] METHODOLOGY: THE RE-RADIATION CHANNEL
[section_chip] RECALL
[figure_caption] The temperature through the ply stack of the reference run, from the hot face to the back face, and the back-face temperature that the insulation holds.
The source: the hot face radiates to the freestream as a grey body, q = eps sigma T^4, with the emissivity of the outer Nicalon SiC ply at 0.75.
The balance: at the hot face the radiative and the conductive paths are the two ways the heat leaves, and together they balance the convective load that arrives.
The numbers: the reference run carries a convective load of 143,760 W/m^2, a reradiation of 140,399 W/m^2 and a conduction of 3,362 W/m^2.
The consequence: the reradiation takes almost the whole load, so the surface temperature is set by the radiative balance and the insulation sees very little of it.

[frame_title] PREVIOUS ISSUE 1: THE HEAT FLUX WAS UNDEFINED
[section_chip] RECALL
[slot_footer] The issue as it stood in the earlier work. The narration stays editable in PowerPoint.
[figure_caption] Heat flux of the smooth and the scalloped body over the particle-time step, as the per-element mean and as the peak cell: the two candidate channels of the heat-flux definition, which do not rank the two bodies the same way.
[figure_source] Source: CurrentThesisFinalReport/figures/fignew_scalloped_vs_smooth_heatflux.png, thesis figure archive.
[slot:recall-previous-issue-1] The earlier assumption:
one heat-flux channel was assumed, because convection, through-TPS conduction and reradiation were never separated.
[card:recall-previous-issue-1#1] The consequence:
the hot-face temperature and the heat load followed from that one channel, so the TPS was never solved for.
[card:recall-previous-issue-1#2] The evidence:
the per-element mean and the peak cell rank the smooth and the scalloped body differently, as the figure above shows.
[card:recall-previous-issue-1#3] The correction:
one hot-face balance solved per element in Ada/SPARK, Sutton-Graves convection in, reradiation out and Fourier conduction through the five-ply layup, so the backface temperature follows from the same balance.
===== slide 17 =====
[frame_title] PREVIOUS ISSUE 2: THE COST FUNCTION INCOMPLETE
[section_chip] RECALL
[slot_footer] The issue as it stood in the earlier work. The narration stays editable in PowerPoint.
[slot:recall-previous-issue-2] The earlier assumption:
the cost function was incomplete, because the objective was mis-stated and the 400 initial samples were spent one by one.
[card:recall-previous-issue-2#1] The consequence:
the shape the earlier run called optimized performed worse on the aerothermodynamics, so that gain could not be claimed.
[card:recall-previous-issue-2#2] The evidence:
the direct simulation of that shape moved the ballistic coefficient and the heat load far from what the closed-form chain had reported.
[card:recall-previous-issue-2#3] The correction:
the cost became the weighted sum of the heat load, the stagnation flux, the ballistic-coefficient deviation and the drag deficit, plus a penalty on the envelope and the payload, so no solver call is needed to score a design.
===== slide 18 =====
[frame_title] PREVIOUS ISSUE 3: THE BAYESIAN STEP WAS WRONG
[section_chip] RECALL
[slot_footer] The issue as it stood in the earlier work. The narration stays editable in PowerPoint.
[slot:recall-previous-issue-3] The earlier assumption:
the search was driven by an analytic model and a Bayesian expectation, so a surrogate of the model decided which candidate was worth simulating next.
[card:recall-previous-issue-3#1] The consequence:
the acquisition step answered the question of the surrogate instead of the question of the design, and the surrogate had to be denoised before it could be used at all.
[card:recall-previous-issue-3#2] The evidence:
the thesis removed the Bayesian-optimisation material and states that there is no central composite screen, no response surface, no surrogate and no acquisition step in the path.
[card:recall-previous-issue-3#3] The correction:
the Latin hypercube seeds the genetic algorithm, every individual is scored with the closed-form cost, and the only high-fidelity runs are the validations of the pass-1 and the pass-2 winner.
