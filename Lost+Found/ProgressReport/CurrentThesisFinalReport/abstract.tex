\textbf{\large  ABSTRACT}\\[0.5cm]
\end{center}

This thesis investigates the aerothermal characteristics of Hypersonic Inflatable Aerodynamic Decelerators (HIADs), focusing on the effect of their scalloped surface topology on flow physics and aerodynamic heating. Using the Direct Simulation Monte Carlo (DSMC) method in the SPARTA solver, the study builds a framework for rarefied gas dynamics analysis of HIAD reentry at transitional flow regimes ($0.01 < Kn < 1$). The framework integrates parametric CadQuery geometry generation, DSMC aerothermal simulation, Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) refinement using DeepXDE, and Genetic Algorithm optimization with a Bayesian Optimization (GP Matérn 5/2 + Expected Improvement) surrogate. The methodology is grounded in the Boltzmann transport equation and validated against IRVE-3 flight data, with the Sutton-Graves stagnation-point correlation and Fay--Riddell theory serving as thermal benchmarks. The DSMC solution resolves a Knudsen number field that spans the continuum and slip-flow bands at the trajectory point examined, with the transitional and free-molecular bands not attained. At that condition the Sutton-Graves correlation gives a peak stagnation heat flux of $16.14$\,W/cm$^2$, which is $+12.4\%$ relative to the $14.36$\,W/cm$^2$ IRVE-3 flight measurement and $+5.8\%$ relative to the $15.26$\,W/cm$^2$ trajectory-integrated value reported for the reference MDAO model. The integrated quantities fall below flight, by $-15.0\%$ for total heat load and $-14.6\%$ for peak deceleration, because the DSMC run samples a single trajectory point where the flight reconstruction and the MDAO model integrate the whole reentry. A grid-factor of 0.7 is shown to be the optimal balance between accuracy and computational cost. Optimization over nose radius, torus radius and half-cone angle selects a geometry with a nose radius of $2.9714$\,m, and the vehicle does not survive the thermal constraint: the five-ply IRVE-3 flexible thermal protection system, $6.35$\,mm in total, yields a backface temperature of $955.66$\,K, which exceeds the $523.15$\,K structural limit of that system by $432.5$\,K. Survivability is therefore a constraint on the material stack rather than on the geometry alone, and that is the central finding of this work. A 5-species air model (N$_2$, O$_2$, NO, N, O) with Variable Soft Sphere collision physics captures vibrational excitation and dissociation.

\noindent \textbf{Keywords}: Hypersonic Inflatable Aerodynamic Decelerator (HIAD), Aerothermodynamics, Direct Simulation Monte Carlo (DSMC), SPARTA, Rarefied Gas Dynamics, Scalloped Geometry, Aerodynamic Heating, Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN), Genetic Algorithm Optimization
